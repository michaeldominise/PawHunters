You mistake a mirage for a smoothie shop. You cry at the sand.
You yell “This heat won’t beat me!” and immediately faceplant in a dune.
You trip on flat ground. The sand laughs. You swear it laughs.
You get sand in places that defy anatomy.
You take a bite of weird fruit. It tastes like regret and also possibly battery acid.
You find a scorpion. You scream. It screams. Mutual trauma.
You finally find water. It's one drop. A lizard steals it from you.
A tumbleweed follows you for three hours. It’s judging you silently.
You get sunburn… on your eyelids. Somehow.
You sit down to rest and instantly get covered in ants. They claim squatters’ rights.
You hear something behind you. It’s your canteen squeaking. Again. Still scary.
You think you're near camp. You are not. That’s a mirage of your tent mocking you.
You dance to stay warm at night. The wind responds with a sand slap.
You name a rock. It breaks in half the next step. Friendship over.
You try to make a fire. The only thing that burns is your pride.
A sandstorm hits. You try to look cool. You end up coughing up half a dune.
You throw a tantrum. A beetle walks past unimpressed.
You lose your shoe to the sand. You give a short eulogy.
You write “Send help” in the sand. Wind erases it before you finish “send.”
You tell yourself “It’s just a bad day.” The desert replies, “It’s only 10 AM.”
